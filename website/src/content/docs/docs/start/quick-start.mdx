This page takes you from the first launch to a first blend between two tracks, played by Prisme itself. To drive the decks with timecode records, set up the audio as below, then follow [Setting up vinyl control](/docs/dvs/setup/).

## 1. Set up the audio

Prisme starts on your system's default sound output. The title bar shows the driver and the device it plays on, next to a green light.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** with the button of the title bar, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> on a Mac). It opens on the **Audio** page.

2. In **Driver type**, choose the driver for your sound card: **ASIO** for a DJ interface on Windows if it has one, otherwise the system's (**WASAPI**, **Core Audio**, **ALSA**).

3. In **Output**, choose your sound card.

4. Leave **Buffer** on **Driver default** for now.

5. Open the **Mixing** page and choose who mixes in **Mixing**:
   - **External mixer** if each deck goes to its own channel of a hardware mixer (a sound card with four outputs or more);
   - **Software mixer** if you mix in Prisme, from a laptop alone or a controller.

6. Press **Apply** in the bar at the bottom. The audio restarts on your card.

</Steps>

Each setting is explained in [Sound card and latency](/docs/start/audio/).

<Aside type="tip">
On a sound card with only two outputs, Prisme mixes by itself whatever you choose: there is no room for one pair of outputs per deck.
</Aside>

## 2. Add your music

Prisme reads your system's music folder: **Music** on Windows and macOS, your music folder on Linux. Copy your tracks there, in as many sub-folders as you like.

The library shows that folder on the left, under **Music**, with its sub-folders. Click one to list its tracks. In the meantime, Prisme analyses every new file in the background: the toolbar says **Analysing 12 / 340** and the tempo (BPM) and key fill in as it goes. A track you load before its turn is analysed as it loads.

Prisme reads MP3, AAC, ALAC, FLAC, Ogg Vorbis, WAV and AIFF. More in [Adding your music](/docs/library/adding-music/) and [Analysis](/docs/library/analysis/).

## 3. Load a track on deck A

Deck A is the top lane, deck B the one under it. Any of these loads a track:

- **Drag** the track from the library onto the deck's lane.
- Press the **A** or **B** button at the end of the track's row.
- **Double-click** the track: it goes on the first deck with nothing loaded.
- Select it with <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> and press <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> for deck A or <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> for deck B.

The deck shows the title, the time remaining in large figures, the BPM and the key, and the waveform scrolls around a white playhead. See [The main screen](/docs/interface/tour/).

## 4. Play it

Press **PLAY** on deck A's rail, or <kbd>S</kbd>. The level meter of the title bar moves; with the software mixer, raise deck A's fader at the end of its lane if it is down.

**CUE** works as on a CDJ: stopped, it sets the cue point where the deck is; while playing, it goes back to the cue point and stops; held on the cue point, it plays until you let go, then goes back there, unless you press **PLAY** meanwhile. Its key on the keyboard is <kbd>A</kbd>. More in [Playing a track](/docs/decks/playing/).

## 5. Get the next track ready on deck B

<Steps>

1. Choose the next track. With **Compare with** set to **A** over the library, the keys that fit deck A's are filled in, and the **Pitch → A** column gives the pitch that brings each track to deck A's tempo.

2. Load it on deck B.

3. Listen to it in the headphones:
   - with the software mixer, press the headphones button of deck B's channel. **Settings** > **Mixing** > **Headphones** sets where they play;
   - with an external mixer, press the cue key of deck B's channel on your mixer.

4. Place deck B where you want to start it: click its waveform, or drag the overview under it. Press **CUE** while it is stopped to keep that place.

</Steps>

## 6. Sync and start

<Steps>

1. Press **SYNC** on deck B. It takes the tempo of the leader, here deck A, and stays locked to its beats: the BPM readouts now match. Hold **SYNC** instead to match the tempo and the beats once, leaving deck B free.

2. Press **PLAY** on deck B, or <kbd>K</kbd>.

3. Watch the phase strip between the lanes: the beats of A along the top, those of B along the bottom. In phase, they meet and the strip says **Matched**; otherwise it tells by how many milliseconds B is ahead or behind.

</Steps>

**SYNC** needs a beat grid: it stays disabled until the track is analysed. With **Quantize** on (the default, in **Settings** > **DJ**), cues, loops and jumps land on the beat. More in [Sync and tempo](/docs/decks/sync/).

## 7. Blend

With the **software mixer**:

<Steps>

1. On deck B's channel, turn the **LOW** knob down so the two bass lines do not pile up. Click a knob's label to kill that band at once, click again to bring it back.

2. Move the crossfader, under deck A's lane, from **A** towards **B**. Or bring up deck B's fader, at the end of its lane.

3. When B carries the mix, bring B's **LOW** back up as you take A's down, then finish the crossfader's travel.

</Steps>

With an **external mixer**, do the same with the EQ, the faders and the crossfader of your hardware mixer.

Deck A ends: its remaining time turns amber in the last 30 seconds and blinks red in the last 10. Load the next track on A, and start again from step 5.
