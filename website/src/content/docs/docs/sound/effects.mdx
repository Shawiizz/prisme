Each deck has a chain of three effect slots, played one after the other. Eight effects are
available: echo, ping-pong echo, reverb, flanger, phaser, filter, gate and bitcrusher. They follow
the deck's tempo, and they can play on the whole track or on one of its [stems](/docs/sound/stems/).

## Turn an effect on

<Steps>

1. On the deck's rail, press **FX** among the pad modes (**CUES**, **LOOP**, **ROLL**… **FX**,
   **SMPL**). The eight pads become the eight effects and the knobs of the effects appear beside
   them.

2. Choose a slot with the keys **1**, **2** and **3**. The pads and knobs act on that slot.

3. Press a pad: its effect goes into the slot and starts. Press it again to stop it.

4. Turn **MIX**, **AMOUNT** and the second knob, and set the period with **½** and **×2**.

</Steps>

A slot key is marked while its effect plays, so you can see at a glance which slots are on. A pad's
bar dims when its effect plays in another slot of the chain. Effects work whether or not the deck is
playing, and with either mixer.

## The knobs

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **MIX** | The level of the effect. For the echoes and the reverb, how loud the effect is added to the untouched sound; for the others, how far the sound moves from dry (`0 %`) to fully processed (`100 %`). |
| **AMOUNT** | The effect's main knob: feedback, room size, depth… (see below). |
| Second knob | Its name changes with the effect: **TONE**, **DAMP**, **WIDTH**, **DEPTH**, **RESO**, **LENGTH** or **HOLD**. In the middle, the effect sounds as it would without it. |
| **½**, **×2** | The period, in beats, from 1/8 to 8: the time between echoes, the length of a sweep or of a gate's cycle. |

Each slot keeps its own settings: the effect, the knobs and the period. A new slot starts with an
echo at half mix, half amount, the second knob in the middle and a period of one beat.

## The eight effects

| Pad | Effect | **AMOUNT** | Second knob | Period |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **ECHO** | Echo: repeats on the beat, both sides together | Feedback: how many repeats | **TONE**: turned down, each repeat comes back duller, like tape; at the top, untouched | Time between repeats |
| **PING** | Ping-pong echo: the repeats bounce from left to right | Feedback | **TONE**, as the echo's | Time between repeats |
| **VERB** | Reverb | Room size | **DAMP**: damping of the room, a darker tail turned up | No effect |
| **FLANGE** | Flanger: a sweeping jet sound | Feedback | **WIDTH**: how far the sweep goes | A sweep lasts four periods |
| **PHASE** | Phaser: a sweeping notch sound | Feedback | **DEPTH**: how wide the sweep is | A sweep lasts four periods |
| **FILTER** | A resonant low-pass sweeping down from the top and back | Depth: how far down it sweeps | **RESO**: resonance at the cutoff | A sweep lasts four periods |
| **GATE** | Cuts the sound in rhythm | Depth: how quiet the sound gets when the gate closes; fully up, silent | **LENGTH**: how long the gate stays open, from a sixteenth of the period to nearly all of it; half in the middle | One open and close |
| **CRUSH** | Bitcrusher: lowers the resolution | Crush: from 16 bits down to 4 | **HOLD**: downsampling, none at the bottom, coarser turned up | No effect |

### How they switch on and off

The echoes and the reverb are added to the sound: switched off, they stop taking in sound and their
tail rings out, as on a mixer's send. The other effects fade in when switched on and back out when
switched off. Changing a slot's effect starts the new one from silence.

### Timing

The period follows the deck's tempo, read from its beat grid: at 128 BPM, a 1-beat echo repeats
every 469 ms, and a change of tempo carries the effects along. A gate of a beat or less starts on
the beat. A track without a beat grid runs its effects at 120 BPM. See
[Analysis](/docs/library/analysis/) for the beat grid.

## The chain

The three slots play in order: slot 1, then 2, then 3, each taking what the one before gives. An echo
in slot 1 followed by a filter in slot 2 filters the echoes too.

The **FX** key beside the slot keys switches the whole chain. Off, nothing of the chain sounds, but
each slot keeps its own switch: press **FX** again and the same effects come back. Use it to drop all
the effects on a beat and bring them back together.

Where the chain sits in the deck:

- On the whole deck, the effects come after the EQ and filter of the
  [software mixer](/docs/mixer/mixer/) and before the channel fader. The
  [sampler](/docs/decks/sampler/)'s sounds go through them too. With an external mixer, they act on
  what the deck sends to its outputs.
- On a stem, they act on that part of the track alone, before the EQ.

## On a stem

When the deck's track is [separated into stems](/docs/sound/stems/), the key beside **FX** chooses
where the chain plays. It reads **DECK** for the whole track; press it to go through the parts:
**VOX** and **INST** for a track separated into vocals and instrumental, or **VOX**, **DRUM**,
**BASS** and **MELO** for a track separated into four, then back to **DECK**.

An echo on **VOX** alone gives a vocal throw while the beat goes on dry; a gate on **DRUM** chops the
drums only. The rest of the track plays on untouched. If you load a track without that part, the
chain goes back to the whole deck.

## From a controller

Each slot answers to the actions **Effect 1 on / off** (and **on**, **off**), **Effect 1 on while
held**, **Effect 1: next effect**, **Effect 1: previous effect**, **Effect 1: Echo on / off** and so
on for each effect, **Effect 1 level** (**MIX**), **Effect 1 amount**, **Effect 1 second knob (tone,
resonance… as the effect has)**, **Effect 1 period (a step halves or doubles it)** for an encoder,
and **Effect 1 period, along the knob** and **Effect 1: the effect, along the knob** for a plain knob.
The same for effects 2 and 3. For the whole chain: **Effects (the whole chain)** and **Effects on the
next part**.

### Focus and the three effect knobs

Many controllers have three knobs per effect unit. Prisme gives them two uses, as Mixxx does:

- With no slot focused, **Effect knob 1** is effect 1's amount, **Effect knob 2** effect 2's, and
  **Effect knob 3** effect 3's.
- Press a key mapped to **Effect 1 focused on the effect knobs / let go** (or 2, or 3): the three
  knobs now set that slot's amount, second knob and period. On the screen, that slot is chosen and
  its key is framed. Press the key again to let it go.

### Super knob

**Effect amounts together (super knob)** turns the amounts of the three slots at once: build a
whole chain of effects up with one knob, and bring it down again.

Soft takeover applies: a controller's knob only takes a setting over once it reaches the value on the
screen. The effect actions are listed with the others in [all actions](/docs/controllers/actions/);
the command palette (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) runs the ones that are keys.
