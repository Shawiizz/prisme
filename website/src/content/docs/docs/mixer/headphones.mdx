The headphones let you hear a deck before the room does. With the
[software mixer](/docs/mixer/mixer/), Prisme sends the decks you cue to a pair of outputs of their
own, blended with the master as you like. With either mixer, you can also preview a track of the
library in the headphones without loading it on a deck.

## Where the headphones play

The headphones need a stereo pair of their own on your sound card, apart from the master. A DJ
interface with four outputs usually has them on 3-4.

<Steps>

1. Plug your headphones into the interface's headphone socket (or the pair you want to use).

2. Open **Settings** > **Mixing** > **Headphones**.

3. Pick **Automatic**, a pair (**Outputs 3-4**, **Outputs 5-6**…), or **No headphones**. The line
   under the setting says where they are now, for instance **Now on outputs 3-4.**, or **Not heard on
   this device.**

</Steps>

**Automatic** takes outputs 3-4 on a device with exactly four outputs and on any ASIO device. It
never takes the surround channels of a computer's built-in card (on a 5.1 or 7.1 card, 3-4 are the
centre and subwoofer of the room), and a device with only two outputs has no headphones: there,
choose a pair by hand if you have one, or use an interface with four outputs.

The headphones keep their pair to themselves: the **Booth** setting does not offer it (see
[Booth output](/docs/mixer/inputs/#booth-output)).

With an external mixer, you cue on your mixer, so Prisme's headphones carry only the
[preview](#the-preview-player). They need a pair that no deck uses: **Automatic** takes the pair
after the decks' on an ASIO interface, otherwise choose it yourself.

## Cue a deck

Each channel of the software mixer has a headphones key, beside the trim and filter knobs.

- Click it to hear the deck in the headphones. It lights while the deck is cued. You can cue several
  decks at once.
- Double-click it to hear that deck **alone**: every other deck leaves the headphones.

The channel fader and the crossfader do not change what you hear in the headphones: a cued deck is
heard there at full level, after its EQ, filter and effects, even with its fader closed. A muted
channel (**Mute the channel** in the deck's menu) is still heard in the headphones.

<Aside type="tip">
**Settings** > **DJ** > **Headphones on the deck a track is loaded on** puts each deck you load a
track on alone in the headphones, ready to cue.
</Aside>

## Cue mix, split and volume

The headphone settings are in the mixer's menu, the **⋯** key at the end of the crossfader, on the
**Headphones** row.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **CUE/MST** | What the headphones play: the cued decks only fully left (`0 %`), the master only fully right (`100 %`), a blend of both in between. |
| **PHONES** | The headphone volume, from silence to +3.5 dB. Double-click it for 0 dB. |
| **SPLIT** | Splits the headphones: the cued decks on the left ear, the master on the right, each in mono. **CUE/MST** has no effect while it is on. |

**SPLIT** is the way to mix without booth monitors: one ear on the next track, the other on what the
room hears. Turn it off to blend again.

The master you hear in the headphones does not include the microphone: you do not hear your own
voice, late, in your ears. To check the microphone or the aux line there, cue it with its own
headphones key (see [Microphone, aux and booth](/docs/mixer/inputs/)).

<Aside type="caution">
With **CUE/MST** fully left and no deck cued, the headphones are silent. If you hear nothing, check
that a deck's headphones key is lit or turn **CUE/MST** towards the master.
</Aside>

From a controller, the actions are **Headphones (pre-listen)**, **This deck alone in the
headphones**, **Headphones: cued decks or master**, **Split headphones: cue left, master right** and
**Headphones level**.

## The preview player

The preview plays a track of the library in the headphones only, without a deck. The room never
hears it and it is never recorded. Use it to check a track's intro, key or energy before you load
it.

<Steps>

1. In the track list, click the headphones key at the end of a track's row, beside its **A** and
   **B** load keys (**Preview in the headphones**), or select the track and press <kbd>Space</kbd>.

2. The track starts about a third in, where the music usually is. A strip opens under the library
   with the title, the time, the whole track, and a **PHONES** knob.

3. Click or drag along the strip's waveform to listen from there (**Click to listen from there**).

4. Stop it with the strip's stop key, the row's key again, or <kbd>Space</kbd>.

</Steps>

The preview stops by itself at the end of the track, when you load that track on a deck, and when
the audio restarts. Previewing another track replaces it. A controller can start and stop it (**Preview in the headphones / stop**) and move
through it four seconds per step of a knob (**Move through the preview**).

The preview plays on the headphones' outputs, with either mixer. If those outputs are the ones the
room hears, or there are none, the row's key says **No headphones of their own to preview in:
choose their outputs in the settings**: set them up as in
[Where the headphones play](#where-the-headphones-play).
