Most timecode problems show on the turntable's panel before you hear them. Open
**Settings**, **Turntables**, play the record, and look at the scope and the four readings
of its panel (see [Read the signal](/docs/dvs/setup/#read-the-signal)). Then find the symptom
below.

## The REL and ABS buttons are not on the deck

The deck card shows the mode buttons only when Prisme reads turntables. Check, in
**Settings**, **Turntables**, that **Input (DVS interface)** names your interface (with
ASIO, that **Turntables plugged into the inputs of this driver** is on) and that you pressed
**Apply**. The title bar then shows the number of input channels, such as **4 input ch**.

The device needs at least two input channels. If it has none, the outputs and the inputs may
be two separate devices: on WASAPI, Core Audio and ALSA, choose the inputs under
**Input (DVS interface)**, separately from the **Output** in **Audio**.

On Android, Prisme has to be allowed to listen to the inputs. If you refused, the settings
show **allow Prisme to listen to the inputs of the sound card, then apply again**.

## No signal

The panel reads **Signal: none**, the scope shows a dot, the **Level** bar is empty.

- Is the record turning, the needle down?
- Is the turntable plugged into the right inputs: 1-2 for deck A, 3-4 for deck B? Play the
  other turntable and watch the other panel.
- Is the interface's switch on **phono** for a turntable? Is a turntable plugged into a
  line input without a preamplifier? Its signal is then far too weak, see
  [Phono or line](/docs/dvs/setup/#phono-or-line).
- With four decks on a two-turntable interface, deck C reads turntable A and deck D
  turntable B: the panel of C shows A's signal.

## Weak signal

The **Level** bar barely moves, the signal flickers between **locked** and **unsteady**, the
track stutters or slows.

- Raise the input gain on the interface, or switch the input to phono.
- Clean the stylus and the record. Dust under the needle weakens the signal and adds clicks.
- Check the cartridge: a worn stylus, or one channel of the cartridge failing, gives a
  flattened ellipse on the scope instead of a circle.
- Check the cables and the headshell contacts: wiggle them while watching the scope.

## The track plays but never finds its position

**Signal** reads **locked**, the speed is right, but **Record** stays at `—`, needle drops do
nothing in ABS and the deck never plays on at the end of the record.

The record and the timecode format do not match. The speed comes from the tone of the record,
which every side shares; the position comes from the bits pressed in it, which differ. In
**Settings**, **Turntables**, check **Control vinyl**: **Serato CV02 side A** for side A,
**Serato CV02 side B** for side B, **Serato control CD** for the CD. The format applies to all
the turntables at once, so put every record on the same side. Apply after changing it.

## The track plays backwards

The record reads backwards while the platter turns forwards: the left and right cables are
crossed. After about five seconds of it Prisme asks, in the turntable's panel, with a
**Swap left / right** button. Swap the cables, or press the button.

## The tempo is a third too fast or too slow

The panel's **Speed** reads about `+1.350×` at normal play, or about `+0.741×`: the turntable
turns at 45 rpm while the deck expects 33, or the other way round. Set **Record speed** in
the turntable's panel to the speed the platter turns at.

## Hum

The scope shows a thick or wobbling ring instead of a thin circle; you may hear a low buzz in
THRU.

- Connect the turntable's ground wire to the ground post of the interface or the mixer.
- Keep the turntable cables away from power cables and power supplies.
- Plug the computer, the interface and the mixer into the same power strip.
- A laptop on its charger can bring hum through a USB interface: try it on battery to find
  out.

## Skipping

The needle jumps grooves when you scratch or when the booth shakes.

- In ABS the track jumps with the needle; in REL it does not. For heavy scratching, REL keeps
  the track where it is whatever the needle does.
- Set the turntable's tracking weight and anti-skate as the cartridge maker says, and keep
  the turntables level and away from the speakers.
- A short loss of signal is held: Prisme keeps the speed for a moment through a bounce,
  reading **unsteady** meanwhile. If the signal stays away longer, the deck slows to a stop,
  as a record would.

## The record feels late

Every nudge or scratch reaches your ears after the time the sound card takes to bring the
signal in and send the music out. Measure it in **Settings**, **Audio**, **Latency** (see
[Check the latency](/docs/dvs/setup/#check-the-latency)), then:

- Use the fastest driver of your system: **ASIO** on Windows rather than **WASAPI**,
  **JACK** on Linux rather than **ALSA** through the sound server. Core Audio on a Mac is fast
  as it is.
- Lower the **Buffer** in **Settings**, **Audio**, one step at a time, until the
  **Dropouts** counter in the title bar starts to climb, then go back one step. The title bar
  shows the length of a period, such as **Buffer 2.7 ms**.
- Keep the master limiter in mind: it adds 1 ms to the software mixer. Turn off
  **Master limiter** in **Settings**, **Mixing** only if you keep your levels in check.

See [Audio dropouts](/docs/help/troubleshooting/#audio-dropouts) if lowering the buffer makes
the sound crackle.

## The deck goes on without the record

The needle reached the end of the timecode: the deck plays on by itself and shows INT. In REL
the record takes it back as soon as you put the needle before the end and the platter turns;
in ABS press **REL** or **ABS** on the deck card. See
[The end of the record](/docs/dvs/modes/#the-end-of-the-record).

## Cues, loops or SYNC do nothing

The deck is in ABS: the record decides where the track is, so pads that move it are greyed
out and SYNC is unavailable. Switch to REL. See
[Absolute and relative modes](/docs/dvs/modes/).

If none of this helps, save a diagnostic report from **Settings**, **About** and attach it
when you ask: it holds your audio setup and the end of the log. See
[Logs and the diagnostic report](/docs/help/troubleshooting/#logs-and-the-diagnostic-report).
</Aside>
