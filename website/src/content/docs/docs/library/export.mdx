A set prepared in Prisme can go on with you: to CDJs through rekordbox, or to any other DJ
software or player. Prisme has two exports:

- **M3U**: one list as a playlist file, which almost any software opens. It carries the tracks
  in order, nothing more.
- **rekordbox XML**: your whole library, or the lists you choose, with the beat grids, keys,
  colours, ratings, cues and loops. rekordbox reads it, and from rekordbox the tracks go to a
  USB stick for the CDJs.

Exporting only writes a new file: your music files and Prisme's library stay as they are.

## A list as a playlist (M3U)

<Steps>

1. In the side panel of the library, point at the list: **Prepare**, a crate or a set of the
   **History**.

2. Click **M3U** at the end of its row (**Export as a playlist (M3U)**).

3. Choose where to save the file. It is named after the list (`Warm-up.m3u8`, or
   `Set 2026-11-14 22h00.m3u8` for a set) and you can rename it.

</Steps>

{/* screenshot: the side panel with the pointer on a crate, its M3U button showing */}

The file lists the tracks in the list's order, each with its length, artist and title, by its
full path. A smart crate gives the tracks it picks at that moment; a set gives the tracks in the
order you played them, a track played twice listed twice.

Open the file in the other software, or drag it there: most DJ software and music players import
an M3U playlist as a playlist or crate.

<Aside type="note">
  An M3U file holds no cues, grids or colours: for those, export to rekordbox. And it names each
  file by its full path, so it works on this computer, or on another one where the music is at
  the same place.
</Aside>

## Your library for rekordbox (XML)

<Steps>

1. Click **Export** in the library's toolbar, next to **Import** (**The library or chosen lists
   for rekordbox**). The **Export to rekordbox** window opens.

2. Choose what goes in:
   - **Whole library**: every analysed track, even those in no list, with all your crates, the
     prepare list and every set of the history;
   - **Chosen lists**: tick the crates, **Prepare** and the sets you want. Only their tracks go
     in.

3. Click **Export…** and choose where to save the file (`Prisme.xml` by default).

</Steps>

{/* screenshot: the Export to rekordbox window with Chosen lists open and three crates ticked */}

When it is written, the window says what it holds, for example **1204 tracks and 38 playlists
written to D:\DJ\Prisme.xml.**

What each track carries:

| In Prisme | In rekordbox |
| --- | --- |
| Title, artist, album, genre, year, comment | The same tags. A track without a title takes its file name |
| Length, file size and type | The same |
| Tempo and beat grid | The beat grid, its bars starting on the first beat as in Prisme. The tempo is kept to a hundredth of a BPM |
| Key | The key, written `Am`, `F#m`, `Eb`… |
| Colour | rekordbox's colour at the same place: Pink → Rose, Red → Red, Orange → Orange, Yellow → Lemon, Green → Green, Aqua → Turquoise, Blue → Blue, Purple → Violet |
| Rating | The same number of stars |
| Plays in the history | The play count |
| Main cue | A memory cue, the first of the track |
| Hot cues 1 to 8, with their colour and name | Hot cues A to H |
| Looping hot cues 1 to 8 | Hot loops on A to H |
| Hot cues 9 to 16 | Memory cues (memory loops for looping ones) |

And the lists, as playlists:

| In Prisme | In rekordbox |
| --- | --- |
| A crate | A playlist of the same name. A crate imported from a folder, `Festival / Peak time`, goes back into a **Festival** folder |
| A smart crate | A playlist of the tracks it picks when you export |
| **Prepare** | A **Prepare** playlist, in its order |
| A set of the history | A playlist in a **History** folder, named by when it started: `2026-11-14 22:00` |

The tempo and key are those you see in Prisme: your corrections if you made some, else Prisme's
analysis (see [Tempo, beat grid and key](/docs/library/analysis/)).

## Into rekordbox, then on to a USB stick

The menu names below are those of rekordbox 6 and 7 in English.

<Steps>

1. In rekordbox, open **Preferences** › **Advanced** › **Database**. Under **rekordbox xml**,
   click **Browse** next to **Imported Library** and pick the file Prisme wrote.

2. In **Preferences** › **View** › **Layout**, tick **rekordbox xml**. A **rekordbox xml** entry
   appears in the tree view, on the left.

3. Open **rekordbox xml**. Under **All Tracks**, select the tracks (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd>),
   right-click, **Import To Collection**. Under **Playlists**, right-click a playlist or a folder,
   **Import Playlist**. Your playlists appear under **Playlists**, the tracks in the
   **Collection** with their grids and cues.

4. Plug in the USB stick. It shows under **Devices** in the tree view (in rekordbox 6 and 7, in
   **Export** mode). Drag your playlists onto it, or right-click a playlist, **Export Playlist**,
   and choose the stick. rekordbox copies the files and prepares them for the CDJs.

</Steps>

{/* screenshot: rekordbox's tree view with the rekordbox xml entry open and Import Playlist in its right-click menu */}

After preparing more in Prisme, export again over the same file: rekordbox reads it again, and
importing a track again brings its new cues and grid.

<Aside type="caution">
  Importing a track into rekordbox's collection replaces what rekordbox had for it: its cues,
  its grid and its tags. Export your rekordbox library first (**File** › **Export Collection in
  xml format**) if you want to keep a copy.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip">
  Before a gig, load a few of the tracks on a CDJ, or in rekordbox, and check that their grid and
  cues land where they do in Prisme.
</Aside>

## What does not go

- **Hot cues 9 to 16.** rekordbox and the CDJs have eight hot cues: Prisme's second page becomes
  memory cues, which you reach with the CDJ's memory buttons.
- **Smart crates stay as they were.** Each becomes a playlist of the tracks it held when you
  exported; its rules do not go. Export again to bring the new tracks.
- **Stems, edits and waveforms.** rekordbox analyses the waveforms itself. The bit rate and
  sample rate are not in the file either: rekordbox reads them from the music.
- **Files elsewhere.** Like the M3U file, the XML names each file by its full path: export on the
  computer rekordbox runs on, or with the music at the same place.
- **Serato.** There is no export to Serato yet. A crate exported as M3U and dragged into Serato
  brings the tracks, without cues or grids.

The XML also reads back into Prisme: **Import**, then **Choose the XML file…** brings the grids,
cues and lists back, the main cue from the first memory cue (see
[Importing from Serato and rekordbox](/docs/library/import/)). Memory cues after the first do
not come back, so neither do hot cues 9 to 16, except looping ones, which come back on the first
free pads.
