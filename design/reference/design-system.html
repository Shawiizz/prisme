Prisme design system

White light for the interface, the spectrum for the music. The rules and the reasons are in DESIGN.md; this board shows every token and component as the app draws them.

01Colours

Three families that never mix: the greys of the glass for the interface, a white light for what acts, the spectrum for the music. The states keep their three colours.

05Waveform

One envelope coloured by the balance of its bands, mixed like light: red for the low, green for the mid, blue for the high. The playhead is the only white line, dispersed on its edges like light through a prism. What has played is dimmed.
